# How to update your website

**Website:** https://tripwithdarma.my.id · **Editor:** https://app.pagescms.org
Works on a laptop or phone browser. Every change goes live about 2 minutes after you press **Save**.

## Log in
1. Open **app.pagescms.org** → **Sign in with GitHub** (your username and password).
2. Choose **tripwithdarma**. On the left you'll see: *Hikes / Tours, Blog / Guides, Reviews, FAQ, About Ketut, Site settings & contact*.

## Change a price or hike details
1. **Hikes / Tours** → click the hike.
2. Change **Price** (numbers only, e.g. `450000`). Leave it empty to show "Price on WhatsApp".
3. Update duration, difficulty, included / not included, itinerary as needed → **Save**.

## Add a new hike
**Hikes / Tours** → the **Add** button → fill in at least *Hike name, Card summary, Duration, Difficulty, Google description* → **Save**.
To hide a hike without deleting it, switch on **Hidden (draft)**.

## Add or change photos
1. Open the hike (or *About Ketut*, or *Site settings → Homepage* for the big top photo).
2. Click the photo field → **Upload** → choose the photo. Use photos at least **1600 px wide**; the website makes them small and fast automatically.
3. Always fill in the **photo description** (what is in the photo, e.g. "Morning mist over Lake Tamblingan"). Google reads it.

## Add a review (real reviews only)
1. Open the review on Tripadvisor.
2. **Reviews** → the **Add** button → copy the reviewer's **name**, **date**, **text** (you may shorten with "…" but never change the meaning) and paste the **link** to the review.
3. Update the **rating** and **number of reviews** at the top → **Save**.
Never write reviews yourself or ask friends to write them.

## Write a blog post
**Blog / Guides** → open a draft (there are 8 ready with headings) or the **Add** button → write under the headings → add a cover photo and description → switch **Hidden (draft)** off → **Save**.

## Change WhatsApp number, address or links
**Site settings & contact**. WhatsApp number = digits only with country code: `6281234567890`.
Keep the address exactly the same as on Google Maps and Tripadvisor.

## If something goes wrong
- Nothing is ever lost: every save is stored as a version. Ask your web helper to restore the previous version.
- If a save fails, check that required fields (marked with *) are filled in.
