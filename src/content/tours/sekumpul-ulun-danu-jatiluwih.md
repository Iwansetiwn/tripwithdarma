This full-day tour links three of North Bali's best-known places in one easy loop from Munduk, with a private car between the stops and a local guide who knows them well.

The morning starts at **Sekumpul**, where several tall waterfalls pour side by side into a deep jungle valley. It's a walk down many steps and back up again, so it's best done early while it's cool. Next is **Ulun Danu Beratan** in Bedugul, the temple on the shore of Lake Bratan with its tiered shrines reflected in the water. The afternoon ends with a walk through the **Jatiluwih rice terraces**, where farmers still share water through the centuries-old *subak* irrigation system.

### Who is this tour for?

Anyone with reasonable fitness. Sekumpul involves steep steps and slippery paths; the temple and rice terraces are easy walking. Bring a sarong or wear clothes that cover your shoulders and knees for the temple.
