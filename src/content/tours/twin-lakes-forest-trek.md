This is the classic Munduk hike: a forest walk between **Lake Buyan** and **Lake Tamblingan**, the twin crater lakes in the highlands of North Bali. The trail leaves the open shore of Buyan, climbs over the forested ridge that separates the lakes, and comes down to Tamblingan, where small temples stand right at the water's edge.

The forest here is cool, green and often misty in the morning. With a local guide you'll learn which plants villagers use for offerings and medicine, why the lakes are sacred, and how the farming and fishing families around the shore live.

### Who is this hike for?

Anyone with moderate fitness who is comfortable walking for a few hours on uneven forest paths. The trail can be muddy after rain, so good shoes matter more than speed.
